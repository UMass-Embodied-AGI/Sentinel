$TaskDescription$

You have just noticed active sentinels on your route. The original path may no longer be safe. Your task is to generate a new sequence of waypoints that safely lead to the destination while avoiding dangers whenever possible. You can look at the aerial view image of the surronding to help this.

---

### Input

**Map Legend:**
- red : Dangerous but transitable zone. There might be sentinels.
- white: Transitable zone. 
- black: Obstacles. Non-transitable zone. Note: the agent itself will mark obstacle area around some known sentinels as a circle with radius of 10. Don't mistaken these circles for real obstacles.
- gray: Unknown area.
The rest is also considered transitable zone:
- green: Your current position.
- purple: The Target Destination.
- blue: Reference route — the original path that may become unsafe.

The map is shown in the attached image.

You also have your numeric position for reference:
$SelfPose$

Current local route suggestion: $route_local$
Current global route suggestion: $route_global$

And all known sentinel poses:
$KnownSentinelPoses$

---

### Guidelines

Generate a **new safe route** from your current position to the target destination:
The route should be represented by a series of waypoints
The route should not pass any dangerous zones unless necessary.
The route should always starts from your current position.
The waypoints should fulfill the following requirement:
- The waypoints should be located in transitable zones.
- The waypoints should not be located in dangerous zones, unless necessary.
- The waypoints should guarantee connectivity. One can easily find its way by following the waypoints in order, with the possibility of lost its way minimized.
- The waypoints should not be too dense. Try not place multiple waypoints along one street where there are no crossroads.
You only need to find a partial route within the map range. Try output 3 to 5 waypoints.

---

### Output Format

Output a **JSON object** listing the route's waypoints (each in `[x, y]` format), forming a route from 'A' to 'T', including 'T'.

No explanations or comments — only the map and the JSON object.

Example Input:

You also have your numeric position for reference:
[15.4, 18.5]

Current local route suggestion: (20, 20) → (25, 29) → (30, 34) → (40, 40)
Current global route suggestion: (20, 20) → (25, 29) → (30, 34) → (40, 40) → (47, 43) → (55, 40)(goal)

And all known sentinel poses:
[
    [30, 30]
]

(and the aerial view image).

Example output:

```json
[
    [15.6, 30.5],
    [20.3, 40.9],
    [30.9, 45.7]
]
```

Now generate your output.
